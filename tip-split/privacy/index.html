Even Split: Split the Check — Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Even Split: Split the Check (“Even Split”) is a tip calculator and bill splitter made by Little Duck. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Even Split does not collect, store, or send any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The amounts you type in and the results the app shows exist only on your device while you use the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Network access

Even Split makes no network connections. It works fully offline.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and we share no information with third parties, because we have none to share. The app store you download Even Split from (Apple's App Store or Google Play) handles the download under its own privacy policy.

Children

Even Split collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: apps@littleduck.com.au
